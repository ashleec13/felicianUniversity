About Felician University

Felician is a private university in Rutherford and Lodi, New Jersey, built around close-knit learning, career-focused programs, and a supportive campus culture. Its two-campus community sits just 10 miles from New York City, giving students a distinctive blend of personal attention and city access.

Suburban & City

Walkable small-town shops, MetLife Stadium, American Dream Mall, and Manhattan just a short train ride away.
